Privacy Policy

Last updated: August 23, 2026

What we collect

Nothing. Quotable — published on the App Store as Quotestreak — is a quote-guessing game. It has no accounts, no login, no analytics, no cookies, and no tracking scripts.

The app

The iOS app ships with its entire quote bank bundled inside it. Your score, high score, and settings are stored only on your device. The app has no server component and sends no data anywhere.

Third parties

The web version is hosted on GitHub Pages, which may log standard request data (IP address, user agent) as described in GitHub's own privacy statement. We do not have access to those logs. The web version loads poster and album art from third-party image hosts; the app does not.
